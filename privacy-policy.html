Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Moment ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use the Moment mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including:

  • Name, username, and date of birth
  • Email address and/or phone number
  • Profile photo
  • City / general location
  • Activity preferences and responses to onboarding questions
  • Photos you upload when completing a Moment

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Send a one-time passcode to verify your phone number or email address
  • Generate personalized activity suggestions
  • Enable social features (friends, groups, feed)
  • Send push notifications and daily Moment alerts
  • Improve the app and our services

3. SMS / Text Messaging

When you provide your phone number during registration, you consent to receive a single SMS message containing a one-time verification code. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Message frequency is 1 message per registration event. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the app (e.g., cloud hosting, SMS delivery) under strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

Moment is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or email.
